Load

TRIMP + body km

HR load = Banister TRIMP, second by second: minutes × x × 0.64 × e1.92·x, where x = (HR − resting) ÷ (max − resting). Resting HR is Garmin's value for that day; the one below is only used when it's missing.
Body load = points for bones, tendons and muscles: 10 per km × (your speed ÷ easy pace, 0.4–2.5) + 10 per 100 m of descent; ski counts 0.4, bike 0.1, swim 0. One easy km = 10.
Strength/HIIT: HR load = half of the same minutes of easy running (1 h ≈ 30 min), body load 0.

Neuromuscular

Short fast reps (sprints, strides, hill sprints) where heart rate can't keep up. A rep counts as neuromuscular when it's no longer than the rep length and faster than the minimum speed. Leave the speed empty to use "clearly faster than the rest of the session".
