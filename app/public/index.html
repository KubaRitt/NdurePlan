What it does

  • Blocks to the second, with repeats, jog or standing rest and per-rep targets: 4 × 6:00 AP2 with 1:00 jog, or 8 × 200 m in 0:35.
  • Heart rate zones per sport (Easy, AP1, AP2, AP3) plus sprints. Strength sessions use RPE instead.
  • Weekly time, running kilometres and load, planned against done, with notes when the week looks too hard.
  • A diary for each day: time, distance, heart rate and RPE per session, plus sleep, resting HR, legs and knee pain.
  • Templates for your usual sessions and one-tap copy of a whole week.
